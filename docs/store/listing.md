**Description** (Apple 4000 / Play 4000)
```
Dig down. Grab the gems. Get out alive. Whatever is still in your pockets when the mine gets you is gone.

Forever Mine: Gem Rush is a fast, one-thumb mining roguelite. Every mine is carved fresh, and every trip down is a bet: one more ruby, one more diamond, or turn back now while the way up is still open?

DIG YOUR OWN ESCAPE
• Pickaxe through soft dirt, and blast rock with dynamite.
• Cracked dirt falls, onto spiders or onto you. Build staircases, knock dirt loose on purpose, and grapple up sheer shafts.
• There's no teleport home. The tunnel you dig on the way down is the only way back up.

RISK EVERYTHING
• Gems are only yours once you carry them out through the surface camp. Die, and every gem you carry is lost.
• Deeper means sapphires give way to emeralds, rubies and diamonds — and to more danger.
• Lanterns in the mine are shops, not banks. Every upgrade you buy comes straight out of your haul.

WAKE THE VOLCANO
• Far below lies the Heartstone, worth 7,000. Touch it and the volcano awakens: lava floods up behind you, and you climb.
• Carry it out to open the next, deeper mine. Your upgrades come with you; your luck may not.

THINGS IN THE DARK
• Spiders dig through dirt and pounce when they see you.
• Bats can't hurt you, but they'll snatch a gem and fly. Knock one down to get your gem back.
• Your headlamp only reaches so far. Upgrade it to see trouble coming.

THE DAILY MINE
• Every day at 00:00 UTC, one new mine — the same mine for every player.
• Unlimited attempts; your best haul counts.
• Finish in the top three to win gold, silver or bronze, shown next to your name forever.

ENDLESS
• Chain mine after mine, deeper each time, and climb the global Endless leaderboard.

ALSO
• Plays offline; your runs upload the next time you're connected.
• Save your progress with Sign in with Apple or Google and take your name and medals to a new phone.
• One optional revive per run, by watching an ad. Say no and you'll never see an ad.
• English and 简体中文.
```

**描述** (4000)
```
往下挖，抢宝石，活着出来。被矿洞吞掉的那一刻，口袋里的宝石就全没了。

《矿世奇财：宝石狂潮》是一款单手就能玩的快节奏挖矿 Roguelite。每座矿井都是全新生成的，每一次下潜都是一场赌局：再挖一颗红宝石、再找一颗钻石，还是趁回头路还在，现在就往上爬？

自己挖出逃生路
• 用镐子挖开松土，用炸药炸开岩石。
• 挖松的泥土会塌下来，可能砸中蜘蛛，也可能砸中你。挖台阶、故意弄塌泥土垫脚、用抓钩攀上竖井。
• 没有传送回家这种好事。下去时挖的那条路，就是唯一的回头路。

孤注一掷
• 宝石只有带出地面营地才算你的。死在矿里，身上的宝石全部清零。
• 越往深处，蓝宝石变成绿宝石、红宝石、钻石，危险也越大。
• 矿里的灯是补给站，不是银行。买的每一样升级，都直接从你的收获里扣。

唤醒火山
• 矿井最深处藏着价值 7,000 的火山之心。一碰到它，火山就醒了：岩浆从身后涌上来，你只能往上爬。
• 把它带出去，就能进入更深的下一座矿。升级跟着你走，运气可不一定。

黑暗里的东西
• 蜘蛛会钻泥土，一看到你就扑上来。
• 蝙蝠伤不了你，却会叼走一颗宝石就跑。把它打下来，宝石就能抢回来。
• 头灯只照得到眼前。升级头灯，才能提前看到危险。

每日矿井
• 每天 UTC 00:00（北京时间早上 8 点）开放一座新矿，所有玩家挖的是同一座。
• 不限次数，以当天最佳成绩为准。
• 进入当天前三，就能赢得金、银、铜牌，永远挂在你的名字旁边。

无尽模式
• 一座接一座，越挖越深，冲上全球无尽排行榜。

还有
• 支持离线游玩，联网后自动上传成绩。
• 通过 Apple 或 Google 登录保存进度，换手机也能带走名字和奖牌。
• 每局可选一次看广告复活。不想看？点"不用了"，就永远不会看到广告。
• 支持简体中文与英文。
```
