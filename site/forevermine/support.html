Support

Questions, bug reports or an offensive name on the leaderboards? Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Please include your miner name and your phone model.

When does the daily mine change?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. Everyone digs the same mine that day, and your best score counts. Gold, silver and bronze are awarded about an hour after the day ends, so runs finished offline still have time to upload.

My score didn't appear on the leaderboard.

Runs finished offline upload automatically the next time the game is online. Open the game with a connection and check again. Runs that break the game's rules (for example, impossible scores) are not accepted.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

Yes, if you saved it first: on your old phone open Profile → Save your progress and sign in with Apple (iPhone/iPad) or Google (Android). On the new phone, open Profile, sign in with the same account, and choose Switch. Your name, medals and run history come back. A profile that was never saved can't be recovered.

How do I change my miner name?

Profile → Change. After your first pick you can change it once every 30 days. Names must be unique, 2–15 characters, and free of offensive words.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. The only ad in the game is the optional revive offered once per run when you die. Tap "No thanks" and you'll never see one.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account. See the privacy policy for deleting your data without the game.

帮助与支持

有问题、发现 bug，或在排行榜上看到不当的名字？请发邮件至 {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}，并附上你的矿工名和手机型号。

每日矿井什么时候更换？

每天 UTC 时间 00:00（北京时间早上 8 点）。当天所有玩家挖的是同一座矿，以你当天的最佳成绩为准。金、银、铜牌会在当天结束约一小时后颁发，让离线完成的对局有时间上传。

我的成绩没有出现在排行榜上。

离线完成的对局会在游戏下次联网时自动上传，请联网打开游戏后再看看。违反游戏规则的对局（例如不可能达到的分数）不会被接受。

换了新手机，进度能保留吗？

可以，前提是先保存：在旧手机上打开 个人资料 → 保存进度，通过 Apple（iPhone/iPad）或 Google（Android）登录。在新手机上打开个人资料，用同一个账号登录并选择 切换，你的名字、奖牌和战绩就都回来了。从未保存过的资料无法找回。

怎么修改矿工名？

个人资料 → 修改。第一次起名之后，每 30 天可以改一次。名字不能重复，长度 2–15 个字符，且不能含有不当词语。

必须看广告吗？

不用。游戏里唯一的广告，是每局阵亡时可选的一次看广告复活。点"不用了"就永远不会看到广告。

怎么删除账号？

个人资料 → 删除账号。不通过游戏删除数据的方法，请见 隐私政策。
