Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the game Forever Mine: Gem Rush ("the game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by HD Expanse LLC ("we", "us").

Short version: you never need to give us your name or email to play. We keep a random account ID, the miner name you choose (if any), and your game results so the leaderboards and daily medals work. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects device data for advertising. You can delete everything from inside the game at any time.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Account ID: a random identifier created the first time the game goes online. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email.To keep your scores, medals and name together, and to stop one player's results from being changed by another.
Miner name: only if you choose one (2–15 characters).Shown next to your scores on the public leaderboards.
Game results: for each finished run, the score, depth reached, gems found, mines escaped, whether you escaped with the Heartstone, whether you used a revive, how long the run took, the daily-mine date (if any), the game version, and when it was played.Leaderboards, daily medals, your profile and run history, and checking that scores are plausible (anti-cheating).
Medals: gold, silver and bronze awarded for daily mines.Shown on your profile and next to your name.

Only if you choose "Save your progress" (Sign in with Apple on iPhone/iPad, Sign in with Google on Android): we also receive the account identifier and email address that service provides. With Apple you can choose "Hide My Email", in which case we only see a private relay address. These are used only to link your profile to that sign-in so you can recover it on another device; we never email you, and they are never shown to other players.

We do not collect your real name, contacts, photos, messages, precise location or payment information.

2. What stays on your device

Settings (sound, language), your personal best records, today's daily-mine best, and runs waiting to upload while you are offline are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game.

3. Who can see your data

Your miner name, scores, mines escaped, depth and medals are public: anyone playing the game can see them on the leaderboards. Your account ID is never shown. Until you pick a name you appear as "Miner#" followed by four digits.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service, which store the data in section 1. Supabase processes it only on our behalf. Its servers may be located outside your country.
  • Google AdMob provides the optional rewarded ad that lets you revive once per run. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

5. Your advertising choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the same form.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch one for a revive. If you never tap "Watch ad", you never see an ad.

6. Deleting your data

In the game: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account, miner name, run history, medals and any linked Apple or Google sign-in are deleted from our servers immediately and permanently, and the records on your device are erased. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove the app from your Apple ID in Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.

Without the game (for example, if you have already uninstalled it): email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your miner name (or "Miner#" tag) and we will delete the account within 30 days. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies; see section 5.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account and game results until you delete your account. Run records may be removed earlier if they break the game's rules (for example, impossible scores).

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Players cannot read or change each other's data: every request is checked by our server before anything is stored.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Most of this can be done in the game (Profile). For anything else, contact us.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.

隐私政策

本政策说明游戏 矿世奇财：宝石狂潮（英文名 Forever Mine: Gem Rush，下称"本游戏"）会收集哪些信息、为什么收集，以及你有哪些选择。本游戏由 HD Expanse LLC（下称"我们"）开发。

简单来说：玩这个游戏不需要提供你的姓名或邮箱。我们只保存一个随机账号 ID、你自己起的矿工名（如果有），以及你的对局成绩，让排行榜和每日奖牌正常运作。广告由 Google AdMob 提供，它会为投放广告收集设备数据。你随时可以在游戏里删除全部数据。

1. 我们收集什么

数据用途
账号 ID：游戏第一次联网时随机生成的标识，与你的姓名、手机号、邮箱均无关联。把你的成绩、奖牌和名字关联在一起，并防止别人改动你的成绩。
矿工名：仅在你主动起名时收集（2–15 个字符）。在公开排行榜上显示在你的成绩旁边。
对局成绩：每局结束时的得分、到达深度、找到的宝石数、逃出的关卡数、是否带出火山之心、是否使用复活、用时、每日矿井日期（如有）、游戏版本和游玩时间。排行榜、每日奖牌、个人资料与战绩，以及检查成绩是否合理（防作弊）。
奖牌：每日矿井颁发的金、银、铜牌。显示在你的个人资料和名字旁边。

仅当你选择"保存进度"时（iPhone/iPad 上通过 Apple 登录，Android 上使用 Google 账号登录），我们还会收到该服务提供的账号标识和邮箱地址。使用 Apple 登录时你可以选择"隐藏邮件地址"，这样我们只能看到一个隐藏的中转地址。这些信息仅用于把你的资料与该登录方式关联，以便你在其他设备上找回进度；我们不会给你发邮件，也不会向其他玩家显示。

我们 不会 收集你的真实姓名、通讯录、照片、消息、精确位置或支付信息。

2. 只保存在你设备上的数据

设置（声音、语言）、个人最佳记录、今日每日矿井最佳成绩，以及离线时等待上传的对局，只保存在你的设备上，卸载游戏即会清除。

3. 谁能看到你的数据

你的 矿工名、得分、逃出关卡数、深度和奖牌 是公开的，所有玩家都能在排行榜上看到。你的账号 ID 永远不会显示。在你起名之前，你会显示为"矿工#"加四位数字。

4. 服务提供商

  • Supabase：为我们托管数据库和登录服务，存储第 1 节中的数据，仅代表我们处理这些数据。其服务器可能位于你所在国家或地区以外。
  • Google AdMob：提供可选的激励广告（每局可看一次广告复活）。为投放和衡量广告、防止欺诈和排查问题，Google 移动广告 SDK 会收集你设备的广告标识符、IP 地址（可据此推断大致位置）、广告互动和诊断信息等数据。Google 如何使用这些数据，请见 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

我们不会出售你的个人数据，除法律要求外也不会与任何其他方共享。

5. 广告相关的选择

  • 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士： 显示个性化广告前，游戏会通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意，之后可以在同一表单中更改。
  • iPhone 和 iPad： 游戏会通过 Apple 的"App 跟踪透明度"弹窗请求跟踪权限。如果你拒绝，你的广告标识符不会被共享。你可以随时在"设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪"中更改。
  • Android： 你可以在"设置 → 隐私 → 广告"（或"设置 → Google → 广告"）中重置或删除广告 ID。
  • 只有在你主动选择看广告复活时才会出现广告。如果你从不点"看广告"，就永远不会看到广告。

6. 删除你的数据

在游戏中： 打开 个人资料 → 删除账号 并确认。你的账号、矿工名、战绩、奖牌以及关联的 Apple 或 Google 登录会立即从我们的服务器上永久删除，设备上的记录也会一并清除。如果你使用过 Apple 登录，也可以在"设置 → [你的名字] → 通过 Apple 登录"中移除本应用。

不通过游戏（例如你已经卸载了游戏）：发送邮件至 {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}，附上你的矿工名（或"矿工#"编号），我们会在 30 天内删除该账号。Google AdMob 持有的数据受 Google 的政策约束，见第 5 节。

7. 保存期限

在你删除账号之前，我们会一直保存你的账号和对局成绩。违反游戏规则的对局记录（例如不可能达到的分数）可能会被提前删除。

所有数据都通过加密连接（HTTPS）传输。玩家无法读取或修改彼此的数据：每一个请求在存储前都会经过我们服务器的检查。

10. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律，你可能有权访问、更正、删除或导出你的个人数据，或反对我们使用这些数据。大部分操作可以在游戏的个人资料中完成；其他需求请联系我们。
